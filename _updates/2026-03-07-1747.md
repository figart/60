Yesterday was an absolutely exhausting day. We rolled off the ferry and into Dunkirk Friday night around 9. Over the course of the next 18 hours we followed a trail of some 10 waypoints where we, according to Tony's custom, would pull in to a fuel station or rest area and message him on Whatsapp to let him know we made it. We might go to the loo (hey, I'm traveling with a bunch of Brits), or fuel up, or buy a snack and a drink, or we might just get right back on the road and head toward the next waypoint. My convoy mate was Trevor, who followed behind me 742 miles across northern Europe to where we're holed up for the night near [Wraclow, Poland](https://maps.app.goo.gl/b5ZdNkKDRwkTmNm18). We pulled in sometime around 3:30 this afternoon.

Fortunately we had no major incidents along the route. One of the gents mistakenly put gas into their diesel vehicle, but that was rectified quickly enough with a service call. All in all a successful day, considering we got this many vehicles this far and still managed to sit around together in the evening telling stories about the day. We had a lovely dinner together at the hotel, discussed the plans for the morning, and retired early.

Now it's midnight and my jet lag is telling me it's not time to sleep. I have a different opinion, so maybe I'll try to doze off again. We roll out at 5:30am. Tony wants to get us to the border early enough to cross... a day earlier than originally planned.
